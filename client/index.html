What you can do here

Three roles, one sign-in, no juggling separate systems.

🎟️

Register for events

Search by category and date, see live seat counts and a countdown, and get an automatic waitlist position when an event fills up.

⏳

Fair waitlists

Cancel a confirmed place and the next eligible student is promoted automatically and notified by email.

🎓

Alumni directory

Search graduates by year, degree and company. Only verified and administrator-approved profiles appear.

💼

Jobs & opportunities

Alumni share openings from their workplaces; students and peers browse and apply through verified links.

🤝

Build your network

Send connection requests with clear pending, accepted and rejected states, then see your network in one view.

📰

Campus news

Announcements with paginated discussion, so important updates never get buried in a chat group.

Choose your path

Your role decides where you land after signing in.

Student

Browse events, register, join waitlists, manage your schedule and read campus news.

Join as a student
Alumni

Build a profile, appear in the directory after approval, post jobs and connect with peers.

Join as alumni
Administrator

Manage events and capacity, approve alumni, moderate accounts and export registration reports.

Administrator accounts are created by the college, not through public sign-up.
